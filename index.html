Rasengan Remake
A Rasengan made of live particles. It sits above your palm and follows your hand. No effect videos, every spark is generated as you move.
Starting camera...
How to use
1. Show your open palm. The Rasengan appears above it.
2. Move your hand. The Rasengan follows you.
3. Make a fist to charge it. Open your hand to release it.
4. Press 1, 2, 3, 4 or tap a button to change the style.
5. Take your hand away. The Rasengan stays where you left it.
