Rasengan Remake
A Rasengan that appears above your palm. Six styles, each with its own surface drawn in code from the anime, and a flat dense wing disc for the three Rasenshuriken.
Starting camera...
How to use
1. Show your open palm. The Rasengan appears above it.
2. Move your hand. The Rasengan follows you.
3. Make a fist to charge it. Open your hand to release it.
4. Press 1 to 6 or tap a button to change the style.
5. Take your hand away. The Rasengan fades out.
