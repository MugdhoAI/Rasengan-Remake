Rasengan Remake
A Rasengan that appears above your palm. Five styles: the Classic Rasengan, three Rasenshuriken with spinning cloud wing discs, and Dual, where both hands hold a different nature at once.
Starting camera...
How to use
1. Show your open palm. The Rasengan appears above it.
2. Move your hand. The Rasengan follows you.
3. Make a fist to charge it. Open your hand to release it.
4. Press 1 to 5 or tap a button to change the style.
5. Take your hand away. The Rasengan fades out.
6. Style 5 is Dual: show both hands. Magnet forms in your left hand, Lava in your right.
